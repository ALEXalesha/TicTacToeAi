**TicTacToeAi 1.0.1: the key hint is readable.**

- The key hint under the "В меню" button was cut off at the top and bottom. It had room for exactly one line, and on a real screen the phrase renders slightly wider than in the off-screen frames the build was checked with, so it wrapped onto a second line that did not fit. The same could happen to the caption under the score and to the game status line; all three now grow with their text.
- New window laws: a wrapping caption is never capped in height, and at the smallest window size every caption fits even with text 10% wider. 204 tests.

Files: `TicTacToeAi-1.0.1-setup.exe` and `TicTacToeAi-1.0.1-portable.zip`. The builds are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first start.

**TicTacToeAi 1.0.1: подсказка клавиш читается.** Подсказка под кнопкой «В меню» была обрезана сверху и снизу: ей дали высоту ровно в одну строку, а на настоящем экране фраза чуть шире, чем на кадрах без экрана, и переносилась на вторую строку.
